# About Me

Hello! I am **LJT**, an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Red Brick University. My research focuses on the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and robotics, with applications in autonomous systems and human-computer interaction.

## Academic Background
- **PhD in Computer Science**, Stanford University, 2018
  - Dissertation: "Deep Learning Methods for 3D Scene Understanding"
  - Advisors: Prof. Jane Smith & Prof. John Doe
- **MS in Computer Science**, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, 2015
  - Thesis: "Reinforcement Learning for Robotic Manipulation"
- **BS in Computer Science**, Tsinghua University, 2012
  - Senior thesis: "Graph-based Semi-Supervised Learning for Image Classification"

## Research Experience
- **Associate Professor**, Red Brick University, 2024 - Present
  - Lead the Intelligent Robotics Lab, overseeing 8 graduate students and 3 postdocs
  - Secure multiple research grants from NSF, NIH, and industry partners (total funding >$2.5M)
- **Assistant Professor**, Red Brick University, 2020 - 2024

  - Published 10 first-author papers during my postdoctoral tenure

## Research Interests
My research explores how to develop intelligent systems that can perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world. I am particularly interested in:
- Self-supervised learning for robotic perception
- Generative models for 3D scene understanding
- Reinforcement learning for autonomous systems
- Human-robot collaboration frameworks

I am always excited to work with motivated students and postdocs. If you are interested in joining my lab, please feel free to reach out via email!
